FFmpeg in Vesper for Windows
============================

Vesper for Windows uses FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/) to decode video and sound. FFmpeg's
libraries are shipped unmodified, as separate DLLs next to vesper.exe (avcodec, avformat,
avutil, swresample, swscale, avdevice, avfilter), and Vesper links to them dynamically. You
may replace them with your own compatible build of the same FFmpeg major version.
